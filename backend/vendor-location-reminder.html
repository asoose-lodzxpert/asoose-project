Hello [Vendor Name],

To ensure customers can easily find your store on the Asoose platform, please make sure your store location is updated using the Asoose mobile app.

Stores without a location may not appear in location-based searches, meaning potential customers may not be able to discover your products.

Step 1: Download the Asoose App

Download and install the vendor app below to manage your store.

Download the Asoose App

Step 2: Update Your Store Location

  1. Open the Asoose Vendor app
  2. Sign in to your vendor account
  3. Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen
  4. Tap Edit Business
  5. Proceed to Step 3 — Store Location
  6. Tap Use Current Location or pin your store on the map
  7. Tap Next to save your changes

Updating your location only takes a few minutes and helps ensure your store remains visible to nearby customers searching on the platform.
